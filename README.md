# SOKO HUB

A multi-vendor marketplace launching in Kapkures, Kenya.

## Launch scope
- Customers discover products and shops operating in Kapkures.
- Local vendors can apply for seller accounts and manage their own catalog and orders.
- Marketplace administrators review vendors and oversee listings, orders, and platform activity.
- Delivery within Kapkures and customer pickup can be supported.
- M-PESA integration will be added after the core ordering flow is established.

## Initial build principles
- Mobile-first, accessible customer experience.
- Clear separation between customer, seller, and administrator permissions.
- Never store payment credentials or secrets in source control.
- Keep the service area configurable so expansion beyond Kapkures can be added later.

## Development status
Project initialized. The first milestone is the storefront foundation and product discovery experience. Authentication, seller onboarding, checkout, and payment integrations will be added in later milestones.
